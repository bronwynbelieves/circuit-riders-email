✦  a little history
Who were the circuit riders?
The original circuit riders were preachers who rode horseback across the American frontier — homies like John Wesley, George Whitefield and Jonathan Edwards — going town to town, wearing out saddles to preach the gospel where nobody else would go. Wesley alone rode an estimated 250,000 miles.
 
The vision now is the same, just with a different circuit: college campuses, and a Gen Z generation that gets handed the reins. Same fire, new riders.
This season is a step in the calling God has on my life for full-time vocational ministry. I'm so grateful for the chance to be trained and invested in, and I am SO excited to see God bring more of His kids home along the way! Thank you so much for considering being part of it!
